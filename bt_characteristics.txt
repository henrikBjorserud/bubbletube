Device Name:

A string made up from: DEVICE_SPECIES (eg 'Bubble Column', hard coded in the firmware) + TYPE (eg '1.75m' set by the pins 7 to 9 on the PCB) + BLUETOOTH_ID (taken from the unique MAC address of the Bluetooth module).

Standby

Data type: 1 one-byte boolean 
Example: (0)
UUID: FB2ADBE8561011EE8C990242AC120002

DeviceBehaviorGenieDisplays Standby state on (on/off) switch under each item in Teacher Mode Step 1. Moving a switch updates the characteristic.OctopusOctopus does not connect to rewards where Standby is set to 1.Power ControllerWhen the Off button is pressed Power Controller connects to all rewards selected by the Target Dial and sets their Standby characteristic to 1. When OnOff is set to 0 videos are played.





Power Controller Dial  
Dial is on Power Controller and comprises a potentiometer with 10 detents. 6 positions correspond to 'Species' of Reward, 3 correspond to the Groups and 1 selects all rewards.
After the dial has moved, if 0.4 seconds have elapses without another move, the Power Controller sends (0,255,0,1) to all the rewards selected and then (0,0,0,1) 0.3 seconds later.
